Policy Cycle

Where each law currently stands

In Brussels you need to know what is happening when. Here we help you understand where in the policy cycle each law currently stands and what the next deadline is.

How to read this: each diagram traces a law's path through all 9 stages of the EU legislative process; the gold node marks where it stands today.

ETS I — currently: Implementation

Next: Parliament and Council begin negotiating the Commission's July 2026 targeted ETS revision proposal (Phase 5, 2031–2040, aligned to the 2040 climate target).

Core ETS has run since 2005 (currently Phase 4). On 17 July 2026 the Commission tabled a targeted revision covering post-2030 rules — a new, parallel process still at proposal stage; it doesn't change the base law's own status.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2001/0245(COD) ↗

Active revision ETS Phase 5 targeted revision (post-2030 rules, MSR redesign, carbon removals) — currently: Commission proposal

Next: Formal referral to Parliament's lead committee (expected ENVI) is still pending; no rapporteur has been named yet.

OEIL's own status field reads 'Preparatory phase in Parliament' — no committee assigned yet. Not to be confused with COM(2026)153 (a separate, narrower proposal on MSR allowance invalidation, procedure 2026/0085(COD)).

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2026/0212(COD) ↗

ETS II — currently: Entry into force

Next: Member states continue building ETS2 registries and reporting infrastructure ahead of the trading launch. — expected 2028-01-01

Trading start was postponed by one year, from 2027 to 2028, alongside the EU's 2040 climate target law (adopted March 2026), citing household/business cost concerns amid high energy prices.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2021/0211A(COD) ↗

CBAM — currently: ImplementationOmnibus-affected

Next: Importers continue quarterly emissions tracking under the definitive regime; first annual declarations are due in 2027. — expected 2027-09-30

The definitive regime started on schedule on 1 January 2026 — not delayed, unlike several other laws on this list. Over 4,100 CBAM declarants were registered at launch.

Omnibus impact: Omnibus I introduced a 50-tonne annual mass "de minimis" threshold exempting roughly 90% of importers (mostly SMEs and individuals) from CBAM obligations, via amending procedure 2025/0039(COD) — Regulation (EU) 2025/2083, in force 20 October 2025.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2021/0214(COD) ↗

RED III — currently: Implementation

Next: Commission pursues infringement proceedings against member states with incomplete transposition; remaining states finalize national laws.

The EU transposition deadline passed in May 2025, but several member states still hadn't fully transposed by mid-2026; Greece, Malta and Portugal were referred to the CJEU in April 2026.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2021/0218(COD) ↗

CSDDD — currently: Entry into forceOmnibus-affected

Next: Member states must transpose the revised directive; the Commission is to issue delegated acts and implementation guidance. — expected 2028-07-26

Heavily amended by the 'Omnibus I' simplification package (in force March 2026). The transposition deadline was pushed twice — substantive company compliance obligations now don't start until 2029.

Omnibus impact: Omnibus I first postponed transposition/application via a 'stop-the-clock' procedure (Directive (EU) 2025/794, April 2025), then substantively narrowed scope and liability rules via a further amendment (Directive (EU) 2026/0470, published 26 February 2026).

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2022/0051(COD) ↗

Critical Raw Materials Act — currently: Implementation

Next: Continued rollout of Strategic Projects and progress toward the 2030 extraction/processing/recycling benchmarks. — expected 2026-11-24

A February 2026 European Court of Auditors report concluded implementation is 'unlikely to deliver the targeted supply security' on its current trajectory.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2023/0079(COD) ↗

Net Zero Industry Act — currently: Implementation

Next: Continued designation of Net-Zero Strategic Projects and rollout of resilience criteria toward the 40% domestic manufacturing goal.

In force and implementing since mid-2024. See the CCUS entry for the Article 23 CO2 storage-capacity target specifically.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2023/0081(COD) ↗

CSRD — currently: ImplementationOmnibus-affected

Next: Wave 2 and Wave 3 companies prepare for reporting under the new thresholds; simplified ESRS standards are being finalized. — expected 2027-01-01

Substantially narrowed by Omnibus I (thresholds raised to >1,000 employees / >€450M turnover). Wave 1 (the largest companies) has reported since FY2024; Waves 2–3 were delayed to FY2027/FY2028.

Omnibus impact: Same Omnibus I amendment as CSDDD raised the scope threshold to >1,000 employees / >€450M turnover and delayed Wave 2/3 reporting; final act Directive (EU) 2026/0470, published 26 February 2026.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2021/0104(COD) ↗

EU Taxonomy — currently: ImplementationOmnibus-affected

Next: Companies apply the new simplified reporting templates for FY2026, with a transitional option for FY2025.

A simplifying Delegated Act (part of Omnibus) cut required data points by roughly 64% for non-financial and 89% for financial undertakings.

Omnibus impact: A Commission Delegated Act adopted 4 July 2025 (in force 1 January 2026) cut mandatory Taxonomy reporting data points by ~64% for non-financial undertakings and ~89% for credit institutions — done via delegated act, not a new legislative procedure, so the base regulation's own procedure reference is unchanged.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2018/0178(COD) ↗

SFDR — currently: Implementation

Next: Parliament finalizes its negotiating position on 'SFDR 2.0' before trilogues with Council and the Commission begin.

Current SFDR remains fully in force. A revision ('SFDR 2.0') is underway: Commission proposal Nov 2025 (procedure 2025/0361(COD)), Council mandate adopted June 2026; trilogues expected toward the end of 2026.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2018/0179(COD) ↗

Active revision SFDR 2.0 revision (sustainability disclosures & PRIIPs alignment) — currently: Parliament: committee

Next: ECON committee vote on the draft report and amendments is still pending before Parliament can set its position for trilogue; Council already holds an agreed mandate.

Rapporteur Gerben-Jan Gerbrandy (Renew, ECON) appointed 29 Jan 2026; committee amendments tabled June 2026, but OEIL's own status field still reads 'Awaiting committee decision.' Council adopted its negotiating mandate 24 June 2026. Trilogues are reportedly targeted for Q4 2026 per press commentary — not yet confirmed on OEIL.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2025/0361(COD) ↗

EUDR — currently: Entry into force

Next: Operators build out due-diligence systems ahead of the new application date; the Commission finalizes a scope-narrowing delegated act. — expected 2026-12-30

Application date delayed twice — now December 2026 for large/medium operators (June 2027 for micro/small). The Commission's May 2026 review said the law itself won't be reopened, but proposed scope changes to cut compliance costs.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2021/0366(COD) ↗

Nature Restoration Law — currently: Implementation

Next: Member states finalize and submit draft National Restoration Plans to the Commission. — expected 2026-09-01

A December 2025 assessment found implementation underway in most member states, but progress uneven against the binding 2030/2050 targets.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2022/0195(COD) ↗

LULUCF — currently: Implementation

Next: Member states track performance against the new binding 2026–2029 LULUCF budgets en route to individual 2030 targets.

The EU-wide net land sink remains well below the 2030 target of -310 MtCO2e, raising delivery concerns among independent analysts. The OEIL reference cited is for the 2023 amending regulation; the original 2018 LULUCF procedure was not separately pulled.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2021/0201(COD) ↗

CCUS — currently: Implementation

Next: Obligated oil & gas producers continue developing CO2 storage capacity; the Commission is expected to table a CO2 transport-network legal framework later in 2026. — expected 2030-01-01

Tracked via the Net-Zero Industry Act's Article 23 (no standalone CCUS law) — same OEIL procedure as NZIA. Storage capacity is growing, but the transport-network framework needed to hit the 50 Mt/year 2030 target is still pending.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2023/0081(COD) ↗

EED — currently: Implementation

Next: Member states and covered entities apply energy management and audit requirements; the Commission continues supporting guidance.

The national transposition deadline passed in October 2025; focus is now on enforcement and compliance monitoring toward the binding 11.7% energy-reduction target by 2030.

Source: European Parliament Legislative Observatory (OEIL) — 2021/0203(COD) ↗

Our Mission

PolicyUpdate.eu is an independent project that aims to make EU knowledge accessible to everyone. In a world filled with disinformation campaigns and public distrust, we believe that citizens need to come together to discuss our collective future.

This is easier said than done when most discussions take place behind closed doors. Which is why we believe that a transparent, bird's-eye view of what is happening in Brussels is essential. Easy access to public information is necessary for democracy to function well, and it should not be hidden behind another corporate paywall.
